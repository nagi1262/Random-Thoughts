# When Everything Is Still

There is a kind of silence that only exists when everything else has
stopped moving.

No conversation.

No footsteps.

No notifications waiting to be answered.

Just a moment that seems to have forgotten what comes next.

I have always liked those moments more than I probably should.

There is something honest about stillness.

When nothing is happening, there is nothing left to distract you from
what is.

Perhaps that is why I chose the name I did.

*Nagi.*

Stillness.

Not emptiness.

There is a difference.

The surface of the water can be perfectly still while everything beneath
it continues to move.

I think people are like that too.

We mistake quiet people for empty people because we cannot see what is
happening underneath.

But there are entire worlds inside a person that nobody else gets to
visit.

Thoughts that never become sentences.

Memories that have no photograph.

Names that haven't been spoken in years.

Things we carry without realizing we are carrying them.

Maybe that is what a soul is.

Not something mystical or distant.

Perhaps it is simply everything that remains of us when all the things
that can be seen are taken away.

The parts nobody photographs.

The parts nobody archives.

The parts that exist even when nobody is looking.

I sometimes wonder what happens to those parts when a person is gone.

Whether they disappear with them.

Or whether, somehow, something remains.

A sentence.

A photograph.

A name.

A forgotten account.

A small piece of work sitting somewhere on a server, waiting for
someone to open it again.

Maybe nothing truly vanishes at once.

Maybe it simply becomes harder to find.

And perhaps that is why stillness has never felt empty to me.

You notice more when nothing is moving.

You notice what was already there.
